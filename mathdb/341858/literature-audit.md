# Literature and source audit

MathDB repeats Conjecture 4.4 in the sole arXiv version of Mircea Cimpoeas, *Determinants with Bernoulli polynomials and the restricted partition function*, arXiv:1902.05302v1. The final article is U.P.B. Scientific Bulletin, Series A, 81(4) (2019), 39–52. Its target is Conjecture 4.1. Both retain the lower index j=0; the determinant definition and order-1 case require j=1.

All 14 pages of the preprint and all 14 pages of the final article were read, including definitions, proofs, conjectures and references. The final conjecture on printed page 51 was visually inspected. The publisher's old rev_docs_arhiva link returns 404. Its current archive for 2019, issue 4, links the working [published PDF](https://www.scientificbulletin.upb.ro/static/pdfs/fullf2e_830874.pdf).

A bounded search used the exact title and arXiv identifier; the author's name with Conjecture 4.4 and 4.1; Bernoulli difference determinants; power-sum Hankel determinants; and Hahn evaluations. It found Diego Dominici, [*Hankel determinants, Hahn polynomials, and a formula of Krattenthaler*](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/s81dominici.pdf), Sém. Lothar. Combin. 81 (2020), B81i.

Dominici's first seven pages were read. Proposition 1 identifies the Vandermonde sum with the moment determinant. Theorem 3 and Corollary 4 on page 6 evaluate the Hahn case; that page was also visually inspected. Setting both parameters in Corollary 4 to one gives uniform weights. The manuscript simplifies this specialization and extends it by translation and polynomial identity. This connection is our inference from the theorem: Dominici does not name Cimpoeas's conjecture as an application. The underlying evaluation is already covered by the published theorem, which attributes the summation to earlier work of Krattenthaler. That earlier paper was not read in full.

Dilcher and Jiu's arXiv:2007.09821 was another lead. Its abstract and pages 2, 5–8 were inspected; those displayed differences use different indexing and normalization. Related 2020 and 2025 papers were downloaded and searched but not read in full. None is needed for the exclusion.

The literal index error cannot earn credit, and the intended evaluation is already covered by the published Hahn formula. This package receives zero new credit. No exhaustive novelty claim is made.
