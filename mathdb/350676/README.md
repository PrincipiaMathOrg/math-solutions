# MathDB 350676: a branched simplicial fan counterexample

The complex generated by `012, 013, 014` satisfies the conjectured gradient--Laplacian identity: both sides equal `lambda^7 + 9 lambda^6 + 24 lambda^5 + 20 lambda^4`. Its common edge belongs to three triangles, so it is not a pseudomanifold, even under the convention allowing boundary. The proof extends to every dimension `d >= 2` and every number `r >= 3` of facets joined along one common codimension-one face.
