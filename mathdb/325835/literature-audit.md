# Literature and eligibility audit

Checked on 22 September 2026. **The mathematical counterexample is complete; new campaign credit is zero pending the remaining source-scope checks below.** This is a bounded audit, not an exhaustive priority certificate.

All nine pages of Zhang, Ely, Aeron, Hao and Kilmer, [arXiv:1307.0805v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.0805v3), were read. The latest version is dated 31 October 2013. Algorithm 1 on p. 3 supplies the t-SVD construction; Conjecture 2.4.1 on p. 4 is the target. The conjecture remains in the freshly retrieved current PDF. Its singular-tube TTN must be distinguished from the sum-of-Fourier-slice-singular-values TNN in the adjacent theorem.

The same authors' later conference paper, [CVPR 2014, pp. 3842–3849](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2014/html/Zhang_Novel_Methods_for_2014_CVPR_paper.html), was read in all eight pages of the official CVF open-access copy. Section 2.4 on p. 3 omits the TTN definition and conjecture, retains the distinct TNN theorem, and says that no clear convex relaxation of tubal rank is available. It supplies neither a counterexample nor a norm-refutation claim. No reason for the omission is inferred.

All extracted text of the corresponding [arXiv:1407.1785v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1785v2), dated 30 October 2014, was compared with the complete CVF text. The complete differences were read: the arXiv watermark, an algorithm loop bound, and the sparse update in equation (20). The target section has the same scope. The CVF landing page describes its file as the accepted version apart from the watermark and points to IEEE for the final proceedings version. A direct request for the separate IEEE PDF returned HTTP 418. Those separate final bytes have not been read or assumed identical.

Zhang and Aeron, [Exact tensor completion using t-SVD, arXiv:1502.04689v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04689v2), was inspected at the title/abstract and PDF pp. 1 and 3–8. Definition 2.15 on pp. 5–6 uses the sum of singular values of all Fourier slices. Its recovery theorems concern this distinct TNN. The complete sixteen-page paper and its later journal final are not claimed read. The relevant entries of [Aeron's publication list](https://shuchin.github.io/publications/) were inspected; the entire list was not read.

The published definition in Wei et al., [Tensor Completion Based on Triple Tubal Nuclear Norm](https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4893/11/7/94), Definition 7, was also inspected. It again defines TNN as the sum of nuclear norms of Fourier slices, and thus does not establish the conjectured singular-tube norm.

The final searches identified a closer related source: Jiang, Liu, Zeng and Wang, [A new nonconvex relaxation approach for low tubal rank tensor recovery](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dsp.2022.103741), Digital Signal Processing 130 (2022), 103741. Its complete available publisher abstract, introduction, conclusion preview and section snippets were read. It directly applies nonconvex scalar penalties to Euclidean lengths of singular tubes. The exact penalty hypotheses and all detailed claims have not been read. The related [2026 model with automatic rank determination](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cam.2026.117762) was inspected at the complete available publisher preview, including introduction and conclusion snippet; it uses a parameterized Moreau-envelope surrogate and cites Jiang et al. Full text was not obtained. Neither preview explicitly states a resolution of the original TTN conjecture. However, their full treatment of singular-tube functionals is sufficiently close that priority scope remains pending. No new campaign credit is taken until this scope is checked.
