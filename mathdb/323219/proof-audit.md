# Proof audit

The proof resolves the full integrality assertion in Conjecture 3.1 of arXiv:1203.4419v1, printed page 16 (PDF page 17). Eligibility is separate and remains pending because the final publication has not been read.

1. The source's Proposition 2.6 identifies `a_(m+r+1,r)` with diagrams formed by adding m nodes to the origin and the r coordinate basis nodes. Its Section 2.5 already gives the reduced-dimension binomial transform and orbit weights. These inputs are explicitly credited.
2. Every active coordinate appears in a quadratic node. If an added node has coordinate i at least two, downward closure supplies `2e_i`; otherwise another positive coordinate j supplies `e_i+e_j`. The quadratic graph therefore covers all active coordinates, bounding their number by 2m.
3. Coordinate automorphisms act on the m added nodes. The image is a subgroup of S_m. A kernel element fixes every quadratic node individually, rather than just preserving the quadratic graph. Loop vertices are fixed; intersections of two edges fix their common vertex; fixedness propagates along edges. The only possible remaining motions are swaps of the endpoints of isolated single-edge components. Those components cannot themselves be permuted in the kernel. Thus the kernel order divides `2^q`, with q at most m. Additional higher-degree nodes may shrink the kernel and cause no problem.
4. The group order is the product of image and kernel orders, hence divides `2^m m!`. Reduced orbit S contributes `(r)_k/|Aut(S)|` to the count. Multiplication by the normalization makes each orbit contribution an integral multiple of an integral polynomial. This proves every coefficient's integrality, not merely integer values at integral r. Empty diagrams, r below the support size, and the prescribed zeros outside the triangle are covered.
5. The top-support diagram is necessarily the matching of m disjoint quadratic edges, with automorphism group of order `2^m m!`. The normalized leading coefficient is one, and no smaller positive integer normalization is possible. The same orbit argument works for any ambient-dimension-independent union of reduced orbits, including symmetric-box restrictions.

The verifier independently adds admissible nodes to the base diagram and deduplicates the resulting sets. It enumerates all diagrams with m from 0 through 4 in each dimension 1 through 8, recovering the corresponding C-table entries. All 87 reduced orbits are exhaustively traversed under coordinate permutations; their automorphism groups, node-action images, kernels, graph restrictions, and orbit sizes are checked exactly. Forty polynomial evaluations agree with direct enumeration. Seventy-five further divisibilities are checked in the source C-table rows m=5 through 9. All arithmetic is integral; finite checks are not the all-m proof.

The argument does not prove the alpha–beta decomposition, the Meeussen Hermite formula, or the Hanna–Meeussen polynomiality conjecture. Those are separate assertions. No independent peer review or proof-assistant verification is claimed.
